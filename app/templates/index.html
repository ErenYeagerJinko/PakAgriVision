Prescribed Treatment & Management Plan

Spray the entire affected plot as soon as late blight is identified. Quickly remove and destroy infected pockets to stop air-borne spore spread. Avoid harvesting or entering rows while vines are wet.

Apply protectant fungicides such as chlorothalonil or mancozeb before symptoms escalate. Under severe pressure, alternate with systemic fungicides adhering strictly to FRAC group rotations to avoid chemical resistance.

OMRI-approved copper hydroxide or copper sulfate sprays. Combine with bio-fungicides such as Bacillus subtilis where allowed. Ensure complete leaf surface coverage on undersides.

Plant certified disease-free seeds/transplants. Practice 3-year crop rotation avoiding solanaceous plants. Ensure adequate furrow drainage and avoid late-afternoon overhead sprinkler irrigation.

Workflow Architecture

Four Stages from Farm Field to Actionable Cure

An autonomous Two-Stage Cascade pipeline engineered to eliminate environmental noise and deliver 80.65% in-the-wild accuracy.

1

In-Field Intake & Crop Prior

The camera captures an unconstrained field image. The farmer can select their crop type (Tomato, Potato, Corn, Apple) to apply hard agronomic prior filters that prevent cross-crop false positives.

2

YOLOv8n Leaf Detection

A dedicated YOLOv8n detector (`models/yolo/leaf_detector.pt`) localizes the primary infected leaf, cropping it with a 5% safety margin to discard soil, background foliage, and sky.

3

Field-Adapted ResNet-50

The isolated leaf crop is classified by our field-adapted ResNet-50 (`runs/field_cropped/best.pt` trained on 8,418 leaf crops), delivering a verified 80.65% Top-1 and 96.77% Top-3 accuracy on in-the-wild field photographs.

4

Grad-CAM & Advisory

Layer 4 gradient backpropagation synthesizes an activation heatmap for visual inspection, paired with deterministic dosages and organic remedies from `treatments.json` in Urdu and English.
