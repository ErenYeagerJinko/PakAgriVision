Prescribed Treatment & Management Plan

Spray the entire affected plot as soon as late blight is identified. Quickly remove and destroy infected pockets to stop air-borne spore spread. Avoid harvesting or entering rows while vines are wet.

Apply protectant fungicides such as chlorothalonil or mancozeb before symptoms escalate. Under severe pressure, alternate with systemic fungicides adhering strictly to FRAC group rotations to avoid chemical resistance.

OMRI-approved copper hydroxide or copper sulfate sprays. Combine with bio-fungicides such as Bacillus subtilis where allowed. Ensure complete leaf surface coverage on undersides.

Plant certified disease-free seeds/transplants. Practice 3-year crop rotation avoiding solanaceous plants. Ensure adequate furrow drainage and avoid late-afternoon overhead sprinkler irrigation.

Workflow Architecture

Three Steps from Leaf to Cure

A robust pipeline engineered to deliver actionable agronomical advice directly to Android devices under low-bandwidth rural conditions.

1

Edge Photo Intake

The camera captures an uncompressed raw image. Client-side HTML5 canvas downsamples the image to 224x224 RGB, reducing payload from 5MB to under 40KB for rapid wireless transfer.

2

Deep Feature Extraction

A ResNet-50 convolutional neural network computes spatial features through 4 bottleneck stages. The final 2048-dim embedding is classified into 38 distinct plant-disease combinations.

3

Knowledge Base Query

Diagnosis triggers a deterministic query to the curated treatments knowledge base (`treatments.json`), retrieving validated dosages, safety guidelines, and organic remedies.
